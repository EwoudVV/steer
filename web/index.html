steeryour steergithub

steer counts how much of your ai coding time is actually you. it watches claude code, codex, copilot, cursor and the others while you work, and counts what you do yourself, like writing prompts, reading what the agent did and testing it. the agent working by itself counts as zero.

i started it on nov 19, the day after zrl posted the ai time tracking bounty. the idea is that ysws programs can count ai hours properly instead of cutting them all to a third. about 10 people use it so far and some things are still rough, theres a list of those further down.

install

paste this into a terminal. it tells you what it found and what its going to change, and asks before doing anything. then you sign in with your hack club account and it runs in the background.

curl -fsSL https://steer.duck.hackclub.app/install.sh | sh
irm https://steer.duck.hackclub.app/install.ps1 | iex

your page at steer.duck.hackclub.app/u/your-name updates while you work. when you ship a project, sign in at your steer and hit submit. you get a review page for the program, and a line to paste into the hours field.

stuff people asked me

"wait would this send my own prompts to you? and my entire sessions?"

no, your prompts never leave your computer. steer reads them there to count a few things, like how many words or if it mentions a file or an error, and only sends those counts and when it happened. for sessions it sends that the agent used a tool, which one and how long it ran, never what it did. it does send your project's folder name, and your page is public. everything it sends is on /privacy.

"if i test it, can i request for my data to be deleted?"

yes. sign in at your steer and hit delete account, that takes everything off the server. steer uninstall takes it off your computer.

"does it support opencode? is it the gui too or only the terminal?"

both, the desktop app and the terminal one use the same database and steer reads that. it also does codex, claude code, cursor, copilot in vs code and the cli, freebuff, and chatgpt and claude in the browser with the extension.

"can we test without using ai to code?"

no, it only tracks time when youre using an ai tool. coding without ai is what hackatime is for.

"can i stresstest this with like 20 agents at a time"

sure. your time still only counts once, no matter how many agents are running.

"how is prompts after you tested something calculated?"

its how many of your prompts said what happened when you tried something, like an error or what the app did. steer checks that on your computer and only sends that it did, not what it said.

whats not good yet

  • it misses real work. on 135 check-ins from 4 people, steer counted about 4 in 10 of the moments people said they were working. most of what it misses is reading the agent's answer and coding by hand between prompts. the vs code and cursor extension is the start of fixing that, and the counting it feeds shows up next to the current one on your steer. the numbers are on /accuracy.
  • it cant see you reading copilot chat inside vs code yet, and copilot's inline suggestions just look like you typing.
  • someone could still script fake events with their own key. the server stamps every event when it arrives and review pages check commits against github, so its harder, but not impossible.
