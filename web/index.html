steer your steer

steer tracks the time you spend working with ai coding agents. the time the agent runs by itself doesnt count, only what you do around it, like writing prompts, reading what it did, testing and fixing stuff. i made it for hack club's ai time tracking bounty, so ysws programs can count hours on projects made with ai.

how to use it

  1. paste this into a terminal. it tells you what it found and what its going to change, and asks first. then you sign in with your hack club account.

    mac and linux:

    curl -fsSL https://steer.duck.hackclub.app/install.sh | sh

    windows, in powershell:

    irm https://steer.duck.hackclub.app/install.ps1 | iex

  2. work like you normally do. it picks up claude code, codex, opencode, copilot, cursor and freebuff, typing in vs code and cursor, and chatgpt and claude in the browser with the browser extension. your page updates while you work.

  3. when you ship a project, go to your steer and hit submit next to it. it freezes the hours into a review page and gives you a line to paste into the hours field.

for reviewers and ysws programs

every submitted project gets a review page that doesnt change after its made. it has the hours, a written justification, checks, every session, and the commits checked against github, so you can check the hours without asking the person anything. fraud squad and program reviewers sign in on the page to approve, cut or reject them.

what steer checks and what it doesnt catch is on /trust, and how close its numbers are to what people say they were doing is on /accuracy. if you run a ysws and want to use it, dm me on the hack club slack, im duck.

privacy

prompt text and code never leave your computer, steer only sends counts and times, and it doesnt record your screen. everything it sends is listed on /privacy. your page is public like a hackatime profile, and you can delete your account from your steer page whenever you want.
